Deleting your Glow account

Your passkey is the key to your funds

Glow is self-custodial: your funds are secured by keys derived from your passkey (in iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or another password manager) or by your 12-word recovery phrase. Breez never holds them. As long as either one exists, you can restore everything on any device.

Deleting your account

  1. Save your recovery phrase first if you hold funds (see the warning below)
  2. In Glow, open the menu, tap Settings, then Delete Account: this erases all Glow data on this device and signs you out
  3. Delete the passkey from your password manager (steps below)

Your funds are not moved or destroyed.

Save your recovery phrase before deleting your passkey

The passkey and the recovery phrase are the only two ways to access your funds. Delete the passkey without a saved phrase and your funds are lost forever. No one, including Breez, can recover them.

To save it: Menu > Settings > Backup > Show Recovery Phrase.

Removing your passkey on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Passwords app
  2. Search for Glow
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete Passkey, and confirm

Deleting syncs to all your Apple devices via iCloud Keychain.

Removing your passkey on Android

  1. Open Google Password Manager (Settings > Passwords & accounts, or passwordmanager.google.com)
  2. Search for Glow
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete, and confirm

Using 1Password, Bitwarden, or similar? Delete the entry there instead.

Changed your mind? While your passkey or recovery phrase still exists, nothing is lost: install Glow again and restore with either one.
