Deleting your Glow account

What account deletion covers, how your passkey relates to your funds, and how to remove the passkey itself.

Your passkey is the key to your funds

Glow is self-custodial. Your funds are secured by keys that exist only on your side: they are derived from your passkey (stored in iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or another password manager) or from your 12-word recovery phrase. Breez never holds your funds and cannot access or recover them.

That means your funds are not "in the app". As long as you have the passkey or the recovery phrase, you can restore access on any device, even after deleting the app or your account.

Deleting your account

Your Glow account is anchored by your passkey. To delete your account:

  1. Save your recovery phrase first if your account holds funds (see the warning below)
  2. In Glow, open the menu (top left), tap Settings, then Delete Account: this erases all Glow data from this device and signs you out
  3. Delete the passkey from your password manager, using the steps below

Deleting does not move or destroy your funds. And no app can delete passkeys: only you can, in your device settings.

Before deleting your passkey: save your recovery phrase

If your account holds funds, the passkey and the recovery phrase are the only two ways to ever access them again. If you delete your passkey without a saved recovery phrase, your funds are lost forever. No one, including Breez, can recover them.

To save it, open Glow before deleting anything: Menu > Settings > Backup > Show Recovery Phrase. Write the 12 words down and store them somewhere safe.

Removing your passkey on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Passwords app (or Settings > Passwords)
  2. Search for breez (the passkey is listed under keys.breez.technology)
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete Passkey, and confirm

Passkeys sync through iCloud Keychain: deleting one removes it from all your Apple devices.

Removing your passkey on Android

  1. Open Google Password Manager (Settings > Passwords & accounts > Google, or passwordmanager.google.com)
  2. Search for breez (the passkey is listed under keys.breez.technology)
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete, and confirm

If you store passkeys in a different password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, and similar), delete the entry there instead.

Changed your mind?

As long as your passkey or recovery phrase still exists, nothing is lost: install Glow again and restore with either one. Your funds and history come back.
