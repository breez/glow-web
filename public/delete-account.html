How to delete your Glow account

Deleting your Glow account means clearing its data from your device and removing your passkey. Here is how it works and how to do it.

Your passkey is the key to your funds

Glow is non-custodial: your funds are secured by keys derived from your passkey (in iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or another password manager) or by your 12-word recovery phrase. Breez never holds them. As long as either one exists, you can restore access on any device.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Save your recovery phrase first if you hold funds (see below).
  2. In Glow, open the menu and tap Logout. This erases all Glow data on this device and signs you out.
  3. Delete the passkey from your password manager (steps below).

Save your recovery phrase before deleting your passkey

The passkey and the recovery phrase are the only two ways to access your funds.

To save it: open the menu, then Settings > Backup > Show Recovery Phrase.

Removing your passkey on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Passwords app.
  2. Search for Glow.
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete Passkey, and confirm.

Deleting syncs to all your Apple devices via iCloud Keychain.

Removing your passkey on Android

  1. Open Google Password Manager (Settings > Passwords & accounts, or passwordmanager.google.com).
  2. Search for Glow.
  3. Select the entry, tap Delete, and confirm.
