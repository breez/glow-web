Glow Support

Glow is a non-custodial Bitcoin app made by Breez. If something is not working, or you just want to ask a question, here is how to reach us and what to try first.

Contact us

  • Email contact@breez.technology.
  • Report a bug or request a feature at github.com/breez/glow-web/issues.

Never send anyone your recovery phrase. Breez will never ask for it, and anyone who has it can spend your funds.

Before you write in

It helps a lot if you include your app version (menu > Settings, at the bottom), your device and OS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

You can also attach diagnostic logs: open the menu, then Settings, and use Download Logs under Diagnostics. Logs stay on your device until you choose to send them, and sensitive values are redacted.

Save your recovery phrase

Your funds are secured by your passkey and by a 12-word recovery phrase. Either one restores access on any device, and Breez holds neither. Write the phrase down and keep it offline.

To see it: open the menu, then Settings > Backup > Show Recovery Phrase.

Restoring on a new device

Install Glow, then either sign in with the passkey you created (it syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager) or choose to restore from your 12-word recovery phrase.

A payment did not arrive

Incoming on-chain deposits that could not be completed show up as refundable. When that happens, a Get Refund entry appears in the menu and walks you through claiming the funds back to a Bitcoin address you choose.

Removing Glow from your device

See how to delete your Glow data and passkey. Save your recovery phrase first if you still hold funds.

Privacy

Glow has no registration, no identity checks, and no analytics. The full privacy policy explains what data leaves your device and why.
